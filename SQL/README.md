# 🍔 Swiggy SQL Data Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is an SQL-based data analysis case study inspired by a Swiggy-like food delivery platform.  
The goal of the project is to analyze customer behavior, restaurant performance, order trends, and delivery partner efficiency using structured SQL queries.

The analysis focuses on extracting meaningful **business insights** from raw relational data to support **data-driven decision making**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze city-wise customer distribution  
- Evaluate restaurant ratings and revenue contribution  
- Identify active, inactive, and loyal customers  
- Understand recent order trends  
- Assess delivery partner workload and customer reach  
- Derive actionable business growth insights  

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description
The dataset represents a simplified food delivery system and consists of the following tables:

- **Customers** – customer details such as name, city, and contact information  
- **Restaurants** – restaurant details including cuisine and ratings  
- **Orders** – order-level information such as order date, amount, and status  
- **DeliveryPartners** – delivery partner details  
- **OrderDelivery** – mapping between orders and delivery partners  

All tables are connected using **primary keys and foreign keys**, simulating a real-world relational database.

---


---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
The following SQL-based analyses were performed:

1. Customers living in Delhi  
2. Average restaurant rating in Mumbai  
3. Customers who placed at least one order  
4. Total orders placed by each customer  
5. Total revenue generated by each restaurant  
6. Top 5 restaurants with the highest average rating  
7. Customers who never placed an order  
8. Number of orders placed by customers in Mumbai  
9. Orders placed in the last 30 days  
10. Delivery partners with more than one delivery  
11. Customers ordering on exactly three different days  
12. Delivery partner serving the most unique customers  
13. Customers from the same city ordering from the same restaurant on different dates  

Each query result was analyzed and converted into business insights.

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- Mumbai emerged as a highly active market with strong customer engagement and high restaurant ratings  
- Delhi showed limited customer presence, indicating growth potential  
- Revenue contribution is uneven, with a few restaurants driving major earnings  
- A small group of delivery partners handle a significant portion of deliveries  
- Certain customers show consistent repeat-order behavior, ideal for loyalty programs  
- Shared food preferences exist within cities, revealing localized demand patterns  

---

## 🚀 Business Growth Recommendations
- Focus on **city-based targeted marketing** for high-demand regions  
- Re-engage inactive customers with first-order offers  
- Introduce loyalty programs for repeat customers  
- Optimize delivery partner workload and reward top performers  
- Promote high-rated and high-revenue restaurants for maximum impact  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **SQL (MySQL)** – Data querying and analysis  
- **GitHub** – Version control and project hosting  
- **Canva** – Presentation and visualization  

---

## ✅ Conclusion
This project demonstrates how SQL can be effectively used to analyze relational data and derive valuable insights for a food delivery platform.  
By transforming raw data into actionable intelligence, businesses like Swiggy can improve customer engagement, operational efficiency, and overall growth strategy.

# 🚴‍♀️ Jenson USA SQL Sales Analysis Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an SQL-based retail sales analysis case study built on the Jenson USA database.

The goal of this project is to analyze product performance, customer spending behavior, staff efficiency, and store-level sales trends using structured SQL queries.

The analysis focuses on extracting meaningful **business insights** from relational retail data to support strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze store-wise product sales distribution
- Identify highest revenue-generating customers
- Evaluate staff sales performance
- Detect dead inventory (products never ordered)
- Understand pricing distribution patterns
- Identify cross-category purchasing behavior
- Derive actionable business growth insights

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

The database represents a retail bike store system and includes the following tables:

- brands
- categories
- customers
- order_items
- orders
- products
- staffs
- stores
- stocks

---

## 📊 Key Business Questions Solved

### 1️⃣ Which store sells the highest number of products?

- Baldwin Bikes leads in total sales volume.
- Outperforms Santa Cruz and Rowlett Bikes.

Insight:
Strong demand concentration at Baldwin Bikes store.

---

### 2️⃣ What is the cumulative sales trend over time?

- Cumulative sales increase steadily over time.
- Indicates consistent product demand and long-term growth stability.

---

### 3️⃣ Which products generate the highest sales per category?

- A small number of products dominate total category sales.
- Revenue is highly concentrated among top-performing SKUs.

---

### 4️⃣ Who is the highest spending customer?

- Customer ID: 10
- Total Spend: 3,780,159

Insight:
Revenue concentration risk due to dependency on a single high-value customer.

---

### 5️⃣ What is the highest priced product in each category?

- Premium product prices range from 429 to 41,699.
- Clear price differentiation across categories.

---

### 6️⃣ How many orders does each customer place per store?

- Some customers placed up to 3 orders at a single store.
- Indicates repeat purchasing behavior.

---

### 7️⃣ Which staff members have not made any sales?

- Staff IDs: 1, 4, 5, 10
- Total inactive staff: 4

Insight:
Possible underutilization or performance gaps.

---

### 8️⃣ Which staff members perform above average?

- Staff IDs: 2, 3, 6, 7
- Total above-average performers: 4

Insight:
High-performing core sales group identified.

---

### 9️⃣ What are the top 3 most sold products?

- Top 3 products sold approximately 290+ units each.
- Sales volume concentrated among limited SKUs.

---

### 🔟 Which products have never been ordered?

- Total products never ordered: 5

Insight:
Dead inventory candidates for:
- Discontinuation
- Repositioning
- Promotional bundling

---

### 1️⃣1️⃣ What is the median product price?

- Median Product Price: 74,999

Interpretation:
- 50% of products are priced below 74,999
- 50% of products are priced above 74,999
- Indicates mid-to-premium pricing structure

---

### 1️⃣2️⃣ Which customers purchased from all product categories?

- Only 1 customer (Customer ID: 9)

Insight:
Very limited full-category adoption.
Strong cross-selling opportunity exists.
